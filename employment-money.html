THINGS VETERANS OFTEN DON'T KNOW

Benefits and Protections That Get Overlooked

Secondary Conditions

A service-connected condition can cause other problems. Examples:

  • Service-connected knee injury causing back problems.
  • PTSD causing sleep apnea or GERD.
  • Medication side effects causing new conditions.

Increased Ratings

If a service-connected condition worsens, file for an increased rating. Do not wait for VA to notice.

Presumptive Conditions

If you served in certain places or times, VA presumes certain conditions are service-connected. The PACT Act added many new presumptions for burn pits and toxic exposures.

Unemployability Benefits (TDIU)

Even if your combined rating is not 100%, you may be paid at the 100% rate if your service-connected disabilities prevent substantially gainful employment.

Caregiver Support

If a veteran needs help with daily activities because of service-connected disabilities, the Primary Family Caregiver program may provide financial support, training, and respite care.

State Veteran Benefits

Many states offer property tax exemptions, education benefits, employment preferences, hunting/fishing licenses, and more. Check your state veterans affairs department.

Education Benefits

  • Post-9/11 GI Bill
  • Montgomery GI Bill
  • Vocational Rehabilitation and Employment (Chapter 31)
  • State tuition assistance

Burial and Memorial Benefits

Eligible veterans may receive burial in a national cemetery, a gravesite flag, a Presidential Memorial Certificate, and a headstone or marker.

VA Home Loan

VA-backed loans often require no down payment and no private mortgage insurance. Available to many veterans with qualifying service.

SGLI / VGLI Conversion

Service members' Group Life Insurance can convert to Veterans' Group Life Insurance after separation. Know your conversion deadlines.

Private Financial Resources

USAA is a private financial services association open to current and former service members and their eligible family members. It offers banking, insurance, and investment products, but it is not a government agency and does not replace VA benefits or legal assistance.[14]
