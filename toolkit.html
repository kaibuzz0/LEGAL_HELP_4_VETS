ADVOCATE TOOLKIT

This toolkit gives you the forms, templates, and trackers to organize a veteran's case, communicate with agencies, and never lose a deadline.

Case Summary Template

Use this one-page summary for every appointment with a VSO, attorney, or congressional office:

  • Veteran: Name, last 4 SSN, branch, dates of service, discharge status, VA file number
  • Issue: One-sentence description of the problem (e.g., "Denied service connection for PTSD; need C-file and new C&P exam")
  • History: Dates of claims, appeals, exams, and decisions
  • Evidence: List of submitted and missing evidence
  • Goal: What you want the agency to do (e.g., grant, remand, schedule exam)
  • Next Deadline: Date and action required

Letter Templates

Records request letter:

"Dear Records Officer: I authorize the release of my complete medical records, including [specific records], to [recipient name/address]. My identifying information: [full name, DOB, dates of service, patient ID]. Please send the records within 20 business days. [Signature, date]."

VSO appointment prep:

"I need help with [specific issue]. I have attached my DD-214, decision letters, and a case summary. My questions are: 1) What form do I file? 2) What evidence am I missing? 3) What is my deadline? 4) Can you represent me?"

Congressional inquiry request: [41]

"I am a constituent and veteran writing to request your office's help with a federal agency issue. I have been trying to resolve [issue] with [agency] since [date]. Enclosed is a case summary, copies of correspondence, and my contact information. Please have your caseworker contact me."

Full Document Checklist with Explanations

  • DD-214: Proves service, discharge status, and MOS. Request from NPRC if missing.
  • Service Treatment Records (STRs): In-service medical complaints and treatments. Critical for proving in-service events.
  • Personnel Records: Show duty assignments, awards, disciplinary actions, and performance.
  • Private Medical Records: Post-service diagnosis and treatment.
  • VA Records: Treatment notes, C&P exams, decision letters.
  • Employment Records: Needed for TDIU and lost wages claims.
  • Lay and Buddy Statements: Witness testimony in support of the claim.
  • Nexus Letters: Medical opinions linking condition to service.
  • Decision Letters: Needed to calculate appeal deadlines.
