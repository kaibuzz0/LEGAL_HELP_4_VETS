About LEGAL HELP 4 VETS

HOW TO USE THIS SITE

LEGAL HELP 4 VETS is a field manual, not a law firm. Use it to understand your options, gather documents, and take the next concrete step.

Start Here

  1. If you are in crisis, call 988, press 1 or 1-877-4AID-VET.
  2. If you need to file or fight a VA claim, go to Claims Battle Room and then Appeals Navigation.
  3. If you are losing housing, go to Housing.
  4. If you are dealing with money, debt, or employment, go to Employment and Money.
  5. If you lost a veteran, go to Widows and Surviving Spouses.

Source Registry and Fact-Checking

Every factual claim, form number, phone number, and legal citation is backed by a source entry below. Each entry shows the source name, URL, status, and last verified date. Laws and phone numbers change. Before acting, verify the source yourself or ask a VA-accredited representative.

  • Verified: We confirmed the source loaded or is widely recognized authority.
  • Pending: The source exists but we have not independently verified it from this environment. Verify locally.
  • Verify locally: State, county, and local programs vary. Call the listed office to confirm.

Disclaimer

This site provides legal information, not legal advice. Before making decisions that affect your housing, income, liberty, immigration status, or VA benefits, speak with a licensed attorney or VA-accredited representative. Do not rely on this manual alone.

Step-by-Step: How to Use LEGAL HELP 4 VETS

  1. Start with the crisis check. If you or a veteran is unsafe right now, call 988 and press 1, or text 988. [1]
  2. Pick the page that matches the problem: claims, appeals, discharge upgrade, housing, employment/money, substance use, widows/survivors, family immigration, state resources, or legal library.
  3. Read the “What This Is and Who Qualifies” section first. It tells you whether the page applies to your situation.
  4. Follow the numbered steps and gather the checklist items. Each page lists the exact forms, phone numbers, and evidence you need.
  5. Use the sample language as a starting point, not a final legal document. Adapt it to your facts and confirm deadlines with a VSO or attorney.
  6. Check the Sources Registry below. Every factual claim links to an official source so you can verify current rules.
  7. Get accredited help when the case is complex. Use the VA OGC accreditation search to find a VSO, attorney, or claims agent. [57]

FINAL WORDS

The system is slow. The system is complicated. The system sometimes says no when it should say yes.

But veterans have won claims, kept homes, stopped wrongful evictions, defeated bad debt collection, upgraded discharges, and recovered benefits after years of denial.

The difference is usually not luck. It is preparation, persistence, paperwork, and the right words at the right time.

This manual is a starting point. Use it. Share it. Update it. Add your own notes. And when you can, help the next veteran behind you.

You served. You earned the fight.

Fact-Check Methodology

Every factual claim on this site is backed by an official government source, statute, regulation, or court rule. Claims in progress are marked as pending until they can be verified.

We prioritize:

  • VA.gov and other U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs sources
  • Federal statutes and regulations
  • Department of Defense and Defense Finance and Accounting Service publications
  • State government veterans affairs agencies
  • Official nonprofit helplines and recognized veterans service organizations

If a source changes, the corresponding entry in data/sources.json is updated with a new verified date.

This is information, not legal advice. Always consult a licensed attorney or VA-accredited representative before making decisions that affect your benefits, housing, immigration status, or liberty.

To report an error or outdated source, open an issue on the project repository.
