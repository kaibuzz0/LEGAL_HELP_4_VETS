🛠️ PRACTICAL MOVE

Homeless tonight

  1. Next hour: use Help Now and contact VA homeless assistance; ask specifically for shelter/outreach, SSVF screening, and available legal help.
  2. Find: ID, DD-214 if available, eviction/discharge papers, benefit letters, medication list.
  3. Do not: assume one program's refusal means all VA homeless resources are closed.
  4. If they say no: ask what eligibility reason was used, save names/date, and ask about another program or grantee.
  5. Legal help: LSV-H and legal-aid routing.
📚 AUTHORITY VA's current LSV-H program funds legal help for eligible Veterans who are homeless or at risk, but each grantee's scope varies.
⚠️ DEADLINE

Eviction notice or court date

  1. Next hour: find every notice, summons, lease, payment record, and court date. Do not miss court.
  2. Deadline: use the date and instructions on your actual court papers; state procedure controls.
  3. Do not: rely on a national website to tell you a universal answer deadline or that a judge must grant a continuance.
  4. Help today: contact local eviction defense/legal aid and ask LSV-H/SSVF about housing-related assistance.
  5. Escalate: if a federally protected issue may apply (military service, disability discrimination, federally assisted housing), tell the lawyer exactly which facts may trigger it.
⚖️ YOUR RIGHT

Possible medical emergency

Call 911 or go to the nearest appropriate emergency department. Do not delay emergency treatment while waiting for VA authorization. If care occurs at a non-VA emergency department, notify VA as soon as reasonably possible; current VA guidance asks for notice within 72 hours, but late notice is not an automatic denial of payment. See Medical Rights.

🛠️ PRACTICAL MOVE

VA denied or delayed care

  1. If it is now an emergency, use the emergency route above.
  2. Save the referral, appointment messages, denial, dates, and names.
  3. Ask your treatment team what clinical or eligibility decision was made and why.
  4. Contact the facility Patient Advocate for unresolved access/rights concerns.
  5. For a clinical decision, ask whether the VHA clinical-appeal process applies; for Community Care, ask which eligibility criterion was evaluated.

⚠️ DEADLINE

VA disability claim denied

  1. Find the date on the decision notice and the review-rights notice.
  2. Do not use an informal “protective appeal” letter.
  3. Choose the authorized lane that fits the facts: Supplemental Claim, Higher-Level Review, or Board Appeal.
  4. Use the prescribed form/online process and keep proof of receipt.
Open Appeals Navigation.
⚠️ DEADLINE

VA says benefits will be reduced or stopped

  1. Save the proposal/decision and envelope or electronic receipt date.
  2. Read the notice for response, hearing, review, or evidence deadlines; do not assume the standard AMA one-year period controls a proposed reduction.
  3. Get accredited help quickly if the reduction affects essential income.
  4. Preserve proof of every submission.
Find accredited VA help.
⚠️ DEADLINE

VA debt or overpayment

Find the debt letter, amount, reason, and every date. Use the instructions in the actual debt notice for dispute, waiver, hearing, or repayment options. Do not ignore collection mail. Open VA Debt.

🚨 GET A LAWYER

Fired or refused reemployment after service

Save orders, service dates, notices to the employer, termination messages, job records, and benefits records. USERRA deadlines and remedies depend on the claim and forum; use Employment Rights and employment-help routing.

📄 FORM

Bad discharge / DD-214 problem

Do not assume “too old” means no remedy. DRB and BCMR/BCNR routes have different rules. BCMR/BCNR generally has a three-year discovery rule with a discretionary interest-of-justice exception. Open Discharge / Record Correction.

🚨 GET A LAWYER

Arrest, warrant, or criminal court

  1. If arrested or questioned about a crime, ask for a lawyer and avoid trying to litigate the facts through this website.
  2. Find charging papers, bond conditions, court date, and jurisdiction.
  3. Contact the public defender/appointed-counsel system or a qualified criminal lawyer for that court.
  4. Veterans Treatment Court may exist locally, but eligibility and timing are jurisdiction-specific.
🚨 GET A LAWYER

Family legal emergency

For immediate violence or danger, call 911 and use local domestic-violence resources. Custody, protective orders, divorce, support, and emergency family-court procedure are state-specific. LSV-H may fund some family-law services for eligible Veterans experiencing or at risk of homelessness, but not every grantee handles every matter. Find the right help.

⚠️ DEADLINE

Foreclosure / mortgage default / utility shutoff

Save every notice and date, contact the servicer or utility through the number on the notice, and seek local housing/consumer legal help before a sale, lockout, or shutoff date. VA home-loan borrowers should also use current VA home-loan assistance channels. State foreclosure and utility rules vary. Open Housing.

📋 CHECKLIST

They said no

  1. Save the decision or denial.
  2. Record when you received it.
  3. Identify the exact reason given.
  4. Find the law, regulation, policy, or eligibility rule they relied on.
  5. Verify whether it applies to your facts.
  6. Identify the authorized review, appeal, grievance, or complaint route.
  7. Calculate the deadline from the actual notice/rule.
  8. Use the required form or procedure.
  9. Keep proof of submission.
  10. Gather evidence that answers the denial reason.
  11. Escalate only to an authorized next level.
  12. Get qualified representation when consequences are serious or the procedure is complex.

🛠️ PRACTICAL MOVE “Get it in writing” is strategic advice unless the governing rule gives you a right to a written decision.
