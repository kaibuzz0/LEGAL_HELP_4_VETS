I NEED HELP NOW

If You Are Sleeping in Your Truck Tonight

Do not wait. Do not try to solve everything at once. Start with one phone call.

Call 1-877-4AID-VET (1-877-424-3838)

  • Say: "I am a veteran. I am homeless / about to be evicted / sleeping in my vehicle. I need help now."
  • Give your location.
  • Ask for: SSVF emergency help, HUD-VASH referral, local outreach worker.

Gather these documents today:

  • DD-214
  • Photo ID
  • Lease, eviction notice, or utility shutoff notice
  • Proof of income or unemployment
  • IDs for spouse/children if they are with you

Also call:

  • 211 for local shelter, food, utility, and housing resources.
  • Your local VA medical center emergency department for urgent medical needs.

If You Are in Medical Crisis

Call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line. Text 988 or chat at 988lifeline.org if you cannot speak safely.

If you need urgent care but it is not life-threatening:

  • Call your VA medical center's nurse advice line.
  • Go to a VA urgent care clinic if enrolled.
  • Go to the nearest emergency room if VA is not an option. You can seek VA reimbursement later.

Document everything:

  • Who you spoke to.
  • Date, time, and what they said.
  • If care is denied, ask for the denial in writing.
  • File a complaint with the VA Patient Advocate if needed.

If You Need Food or Money Immediately

  • SNAP (food stamps): Apply through your state social services agency. Veterans are often eligible.
  • Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF): State-administered cash assistance.
  • Unemployment: File with your state unemployment office.
  • VA pension: If wartime veteran with limited income.
  • SSDI/SSI: If your disabilities prevent work.
  • SSVF: May cover rent, utilities, deposits, moving costs.
