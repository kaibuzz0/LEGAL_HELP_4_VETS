Sources Registry

Every factual claim, form number, phone number, and legal citation on this site links to one of the sources below. Entries include the source name, the specific claim, a link, verification status, and last-verified date.

If you spot an outdated source or a broken link, open an issue on the project repository or contact a VA-accredited representative before relying on the information for a claim or appeal. [57]

How to use this registry: Each inline citation like [17] on the site links to the matching entry below. Click any “Source” link to view the official page.

Methodology

  1. Source-before-claim: Every new factual claim, form number, phone number, or legal citation gets a registry entry before it appears on a content page.
  2. Official sources preferred: VA.gov, benefits.va.gov, DFAS.mil, SSA.gov, USCIS.gov, HUD.gov, eCFR, Cornell LII, and official court/legislative sources.
  3. Verify locally: State and local program details change frequently; those entries are marked as verified only when the URL loaded successfully from this environment, otherwise pending.
  4. No invented facts: No success rates, no promises of outcomes, no “VA always denies X” claims.
