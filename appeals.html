Fight a VA Denial: Pick the Right Review Lane

DO THIS FIRST

Last legally verified: 2026-08-27
Primary authorities checked: 38 CFR § 3.2500 · 38 CFR § 3.2501 · VA Forms 20-0995, 20-0996, 10182, 20-0998
State-specific warning: Not applicable to the federal VA review system; other benefit programs can use different procedures.
⚠️ DEADLINE

When a VA decision arrives

  1. Find the date on the decision notice.
  2. Read the review-rights notice included with that decision.
  3. Calendar the exact deadline shown there.
  4. Choose an authorized review option.
  5. Use the prescribed VA form or VA online process.
  6. Keep a complete copy and proof VA received it.

Do not rely on a generic “protective appeal,” “intent to appeal,” or informal letter to preserve an Appeals Modernization Act review deadline.

Choose the review option

OptionUse it whenEvidenceForm
Supplemental ClaimYou have new and relevant evidence, or another qualifying basis for readjudication.New and relevant evidence is generally required for readjudication.📄 FORM VA Form 20-0995 [19]
Higher-Level ReviewYou want a higher-level reviewer to review the existing record for an error or difference of opinion.No new evidence.📄 FORM VA Form 20-0996 [20]

⚠️ DEADLINE

One year is common — but do not assume it applies to everything

Under the modernized review system, Higher-Level Review and Board Appeal are generally elected within one year of the decision notice. A Supplemental Claim may generally be filed later, but filing it within the applicable continuous-pursuit period can matter greatly to an earlier effective date. Simultaneously contested claims use different, shorter rules. [78]

Follow the deadline and review instructions printed in your actual decision notice.

📚 AUTHORITY 38 CFR § 3.2500(a), (c), (g), (h); 38 CFR § 3.2501.

Supplemental Claim — VA Form 20-0995

⚖️ YOUR RIGHT A Supplemental Claim can generally be filed after VA has issued a decision, including after the one-year period. Filing later can break continuous pursuit and affect the earliest available effective date.

  1. Identify the issue and prior decision.
  2. Submit or identify new and relevant evidence, unless another qualifying rule applies.
  3. Use VA Form 20-0995 or the current VA online process where available.
  4. Keep the complete submission and proof of receipt.

📚 AUTHORITY 38 CFR §§ 3.2500, 3.2501; VA Supplemental Claims guidance. [19]

Higher-Level Review — VA Form 20-0996

⚖️ YOUR RIGHT HLR is a review of the existing record by a higher-level reviewer. The reviewer determines whether an error or difference of opinion changes the decision. You do not have to prove clear and unmistakable error (CUE) simply to request HLR.

  • No new evidence is considered in HLR.
  • An informal conference is optional and does not let you add new evidence.
  • HLR is generally requested within one year of the applicable initial or Supplemental Claim decision.

📚 AUTHORITY Current VA Higher-Level Review guidance; VA Form 20-0996. [20]

Board Appeal — VA Form 10182

DocketWhat the Board reviews
Direct ReviewNo hearing and no new evidence; the Board reviews the record allowed for that docket.
Evidence SubmissionYou may submit evidence with the appeal or within 90 days after VA receives the Board Appeal request.
HearingYou may testify and may submit evidence at the hearing or within the applicable 90-day period after the hearing.

VA revised Form 10182 in 2026. Use the current form or current online process rather than an old saved copy.

📚 AUTHORITY Current VA Form 10182 and Board Appeal guidance. [21]

📋 CHECKLIST

Proof you should keep

  • VA decision notice and review-rights notice
  • Complete copy of the form and every attachment
  • VA.gov submission confirmation
  • Certified-mail receipt if mailed
  • Fax transmission receipt if faxed
  • Any delivery or intake confirmation
  • A dated log of follow-up calls

If they say no

🛠️ PRACTICAL MOVE Read the new decision first. The next authorized review option depends on what type of decision was issued and what review route you just used.

  • After a Supplemental Claim decision, HLR, Board Appeal, or another Supplemental Claim may be available if the requirements are met.
  • After HLR, a Supplemental Claim or Board Appeal may be available; a second HLR on the same issue is generally not.
  • After a Board decision, a Supplemental Claim with new and relevant evidence or an appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims may be available. A CAVC appeal generally has a 120-day filing period from the Board decision.

🚨 GET A LAWYER Strongly consider an accredited veterans-law attorney for a CAVC appeal, unusually complex effective-date dispute, or contested claim.

YOUR NEXT ACTION: Put the decision notice in front of you, read the attached review-rights instructions, mark the deadline, and use the prescribed review form. Do not substitute an informal letter for the required AMA review request.
