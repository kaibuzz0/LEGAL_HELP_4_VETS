What this site is

LEGAL HELP 4 VETS is a free, open-source Veteran Rights Field Manual. It helps U.S. veterans, service members, families, caregivers, and advocates navigate VA benefits, disability claims, appeals, legal protections, emergency resources, and veteran support systems.

Everything here is legal information, not legal advice. Laws, deadlines, and procedures change. For your specific situation, consult an accredited VSO attorney, or legal aid organization.
