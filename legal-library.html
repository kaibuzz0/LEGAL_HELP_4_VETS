Letters and Laws: What to Say and How to Cite

LEGAL LANGUAGE TRANSLATOR AND REFERENCE LIBRARY

VA and court systems use words that sound simple but have specific legal meanings. Misunderstanding a term can cost you benefits or delay a case for years.

What This Is and Who Qualifies

The legal library translates the words veterans hear from VA, creditors, landlords, employers, and courts into plain language. It also collects the statutes, regulations, and sample letters you can use when writing demands or appeals. [96]

Who qualifies: Any veteran, family member, advocate, or VSO who needs to understand a legal term, cite a law, or draft a letter. The information here is legal information, not legal advice. [13]

How to Use This Library in an Appeal or Demand Letter

  1. Find the term, statute, or issue that matches your problem.
  2. Copy the citation exactly, including the U.S.C. or C.F.R. title and section.
  3. In your letter, state the law and then state how it applies to your facts. Do not just quote the law.
  4. Attach a copy of the source from a government website if possible.
  5. Send certified mail, return receipt requested, and keep a copy.

Sample Demand Letters You Can Adapt

1. SCRA Interest-Rate Reduction Request

[Date]
[Creditor name and address]

Re: Account [number] — Servicemembers Civil Relief Act rate reduction request

I am writing to request that you reduce the interest rate on the above account to 6% per annum under 50 U.S.C. § 3937, the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act. I entered active duty on [date] and am attaching a copy of my orders. This debt was incurred before my active-duty service. Please apply the 6% cap retroactive to the date I began active duty and adjust my account accordingly. Please confirm in writing within 30 days.

Sincerely,
[Name, address, phone, email]

Send to: The creditor’s customer service or legal department. Include a copy of your orders. [92] [35]

2. Debt Validation Demand

[Date]
[Debt collector name and address]

I dispute the debt you are attempting to collect and request validation under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1692g. Please provide: (1) the name and address of the original creditor and the account number; (2) the current balance and an itemized payment history; (3) a copy of the original contract or judgment; and (4) proof that you are licensed and authorized to collect this debt in my state. Until this information is provided, cease all collection activity. This letter is not an acknowledgment of the debt.

Sincerely,
[Name, address]

Send within 30 days of first collection notice, certified mail, return receipt requested. [93] [43]

3. Reasonable Accommodation in Housing

[Date]
[Landlord / property manager name and address]

Re: [Property address]

I am a tenant at the above property. I have a disability-related need for the following reasonable accommodation: [service animal / reserved accessible parking / first-floor unit / grab bars / modified lease term / etc.]. This request is made under the Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. § 3604(f). I am attaching [medical letter / VA disability letter] in support. Please respond in writing within 10 business days. If you deny this request, please explain the specific undue burden or fundamental alteration.

Sincerely,
[Name, address, phone]

Send to: Landlord or HOA in writing. Keep a copy. [36]

4. Records Request (VA or Private)

[Date]
[Records custodian name and address]

Re: Request for records of [veteran’s full name, DOB, last 4 SSN]

Under the Privacy Act and applicable VA / medical records access rules, I request a complete copy of the following records: [service treatment records / C-file / VA medical records / private treatment records from ___ to ___]. I authorize release to [myself / my accredited representative]. Please provide the records within 20 business days. If you require a fee, notify me before processing.

Sincerely,
[Name, signature, date]

For VA C-file: Use VA Form 20-10206. [23]

Key Terms and How to Use Them

  • Service-connected: A disability VA has linked to military service. Use in claims and appeals to argue for compensation.
  • Nexus: The medical or legal link between service and a current condition. Use to win claims where the connection is not obvious.
  • Effective date: The date VA uses to start paying benefits. Protect it with intent-to-file and timely appeals.
  • C&P exam: Compensation and Pension exam. Use it to support your rating; challenge it if it is incomplete or wrong.
  • Presumptive: VA presumes a condition is service-connected if you served in a specific place/time. [27]
  • TDIU: Total Disability based on Individual Unemployability. [26]
  • Benefit of the doubt: When evidence is evenly balanced, VA may need to resolve doubt in the veteran's favor. [75]

LEGAL REFERENCE LIBRARY

These are the statutes, regulations, and programs behind the claims in this manual. Each entry includes a practical note on how to use it.

Federal Statutes and Regulations — How to Use Them

  • 38 U.S.C. (Veterans' Benefits): The main federal law for VA benefits. Use it to argue entitlement to compensation, pension, education, and housing benefits.
  • 38 C.F.R. Part 3: VA regulations on claims adjudication. Key sections include 3.159 (duty to assist), 3.303 (service connection), 3.307/3.309 (presumptions). [37] [42]
  • 38 U.S.C. 5107(b): Benefit of the doubt rule. Use when evidence is in approximate balance. [75]
  • 50 U.S.C. SCRA (3901–4043): Protects active-duty servicemembers from default judgments, interest spikes, lease penalties, and foreclosure. [35]
  • 42 U.S.C. 3604(f) — Fair Housing Act: Prohibits disability discrimination in housing. Use for reasonable accommodation requests. [36]
  • 15 U.S.C. 1692 — FDCPA: Gives consumers the right to dispute and validate debt within 30 days. [43]
  • PACT Act: Adds presumptive conditions for toxic exposure. Use to claim conditions without a nexus letter if you served in a covered location. [27]

Sample Legal Demand Letters

SCRA interest rate reduction: [35]

"I request the 6% interest rate cap on account [number] under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act, 50 U.S.C. 3937. I entered active duty on [date]. Enclosed are my orders. Reduce the rate to 6% retroactively and credit excess interest. Respond within 30 days."

FDCPA debt validation: [43]

"I dispute the debt referenced in your [letter/call] dated [date] and request validation under 15 U.S.C. 1692g. Provide the original creditor, account number, itemized amount owed, proof I owe the debt, and authority to collect. Cease collection until validation is provided."

Reasonable accommodation: [36]

"I am a tenant at [address] with a disability-related need for [specific accommodation]. Under the Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604(f), I request this reasonable accommodation. I have attached [medical/provider verification]. Please respond within 10 business days."
How to Use a Citation in an Appeal
"Under 38 C.F.R. 3.159, VA has a duty to assist the claimant in obtaining relevant records. VA did not obtain the service treatment records from [facility] that I identified. Without those records, VA cannot properly evaluate my claim for [condition]."
"The PACT Act added [condition] as a presumptive condition for veterans who served in [covered location]. My DD-214 shows service in [location] during [dates]. Service connection is presumed and no separate medical nexus is required."
"The evidence is in approximate balance. Under 38 U.S.C. 5107(b), VA may need to resolve reasonable doubt in the veteran's favor. The in-service event, current diagnosis, and lay statements support service connection."
YOUR NEXT ACTION: If you are appealing a denial, identify the regulation VA cited against you and find the argument that turns it in your favor. A VSO or attorney can help.
