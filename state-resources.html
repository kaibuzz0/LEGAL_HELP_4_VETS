STATE & LOCAL VETERAN RESOURCES

This directory points to each state's veterans affairs office and flags where legal aid and veterans court information still needs local confirmation. URLs marked verified loaded successfully at last check; URLs marked pending could not be confirmed from this environment and should be checked locally.

Always confirm the current URL and services with the state office before acting. State names, agencies, and phone numbers change.

National First Points of Contact

  • VA.gov — federal benefits, health care, and facility locator.
  • Veterans Crisis Line: 988, press 1
  • VA Benefits Hotline: 1-800-827-1000
