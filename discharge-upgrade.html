KNOW YOUR STATUS

Service Type

StatusWhat It Means
Active dutyFull-time service. Most federal veteran benefits require you to have completed the service obligation and received a qualifying discharge.
National GuardState-controlled unless federalized. Federal VA benefits usually require federal active service. State benefits may still apply.
ReservePart-time unless activated. Benefits depend on active-duty periods, activation orders, and character of discharge.
Combat veteranMay qualify for enhanced VA health care, presumptions, and expedited claims.
Recently separatedYou may still have SCRA protections, transitional health care, and deadlines that are running now.

Discharge Type

CharacterizationEffect on VA Benefits
HonorableFull eligibility for VA benefits.
General (Under Honorable Conditions)Usually eligible for most VA benefits.
Other Than HonorableMay be barred, but VA can still make a Character of Discharge determination. File anyway.
Bad ConductOften barred. Apply for upgrade and for VA COD determination.
DishonorableGenerally barred. Seek upgrade and legal help.

Health and Disability

SituationPossible Path
Injury or illness during serviceFile a VA disability compensation claim.
Condition existed before service but got worseFile for aggravation of pre-existing condition.
Condition caused by a service-connected disabilityFile for secondary service connection.
Cannot work due to service-connected disabilitiesFile for Total Disability based on Individual Unemployability (TDIU).
Needs help with daily livingLook into VA Aid and Attendance or caregiver support.

Exposure History

ExposurePossible Benefit
Agent OrangePresumptive conditions for Vietnam, Korea DMZ, and certain other locations.
Burn pits / airborne hazardsPACT Act creates presumptions for many respiratory and cancers.
Gulf War exposuresPresumptive chronic multisymptom illness.
Camp Lejeune waterVA health care and compensation for covered conditions.
Radiation, asbestos, other toxic exposuresSpecific presumptions and claims possible.

Route Yourself

If you are recently separated, homeless, and have an Other Than Honorable discharge:

  • Call 1-877-4AID-VET for housing help.
  • File a VA claim anyway to force a Character of Discharge determination.
  • Apply for a discharge upgrade.

If you are combat veteran with undiagnosed health problems:

  • Enroll in VA health care as a combat veteran.
  • Get screened for PACT Act conditions.
  • File for presumptive service connection if a condition matches.

If you cannot work:

  • File for VA disability if not already service-connected.
  • File for TDIU if already service-connected.
  • Apply for SSDI through Social Security.
