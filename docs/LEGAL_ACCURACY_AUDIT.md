# Legal Accuracy Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-27

Core rule: aggressive about finding lawful remedies; conservative about claiming a remedy exists.

## appeals.html

**Last verified:** 2026-08-27  
**Primary authority:** 38 CFR §§ 3.2500, 3.2501; current VA Forms 20-0995, 20-0996, 10182, 20-0998; current VA decision-review guidance.

### Informal protective appeal letter

**Prior site text:** A short signed “protective claim” / “I intend to appeal” letter could preserve the AMA deadline/effective date.

**Verdict:** Incorrect / dangerous.

**Change:** Removed. The page now directs users to the decision notice, actual review-rights notice, applicable deadline, authorized review option, prescribed form/online process, and proof of receipt.

### Supplemental Claim deadline

**Verdict:** Needed qualification.

**Change:** Clarified that a Supplemental Claim can generally be filed after one year, while timely filing within continuous pursuit may matter to an earlier effective date.

### Higher-Level Review / CUE

**Verdict:** Needed qualification.

**Change:** HLR is described as existing-record review for error or difference of opinion. CUE is not presented as a prerequisite to HLR.

### Board evidence windows

**Verdict:** Verified against current VA Form 10182 and Board guidance.

**Change:** Direct Review, Evidence Submission, and Hearing windows are distinguished. Old absolute processing-time promises were removed.

### Deadlines checked

- General one-year AMA election rule: checked.
- Supplemental Claim may be filed later: checked.
- Simultaneously contested claim exception: checked.
- CAVC 120-day route after Board decision: retained with lawyer escalation.

## discharge-upgrade.html

**Last verified:** 2026-08-27  
**Primary authority:** 10 U.S.C. § 1552(b); current VA discharge-upgrade instructions; current VA Character of Discharge guidance; DD Forms 293 and 149.

### BCMR/BCNR “no fixed deadline”

**Prior site text:** BCMR/BCNR has “no strict deadline” / “no fixed deadline.”

**Verdict:** Incorrect.

**Change:** Replaced with the general 3-year discovery rule in 10 U.S.C. § 1552(b), plus the discretionary interest-of-justice waiver.

### DD Form 149

**Verdict:** Prior description was too narrow.

**Change:** Clarified that DD Form 149 is a broader military-record correction application, not merely “the form for discharges older than 15 years.”

### Character of Discharge

**Verdict:** Prior wording overstated the effect of filing and contained malformed text.

**Change:** Clarified that VA may make a Character of Discharge determination for VA-benefit eligibility and that the VA determination does not itself change the military discharge.

## claims.html

**Last verified:** 2026-08-27  
**Primary authority:** 38 CFR §§ 3.155, 3.400; current VA Form 21-0966 guidance; current VA C&P / claims guidance.

### Intent to File “locks an effective date”

**Verdict:** Overstated.

**Change:** Clarified that a valid Intent to File can establish an earlier potential filing/effective-date position when followed by a qualifying complete claim within the required period, subject to effective-date rules and exceptions. Clarified that the ITF rule does not apply to Supplemental Claims.

### Informal protective claim letter

**Verdict:** Unsafe under the current prescribed-form system.

**Change:** Removed sample protective-claim letter and replaced it with the valid regulatory filing methods.

### Negative medical opinion

**Prior site text:** A doctor saying “not related to service” ends the claim unless there is contrary evidence.

**Verdict:** Incorrect / overstated.

**Change:** Rewritten to explain that a negative opinion is evidence, not automatic termination, and to identify common adequacy/weight issues without promising a new exam.

### “Describe your worst days”

**Verdict:** Risk of encouraging distorted reporting.

**Change:** Replaced with instructions to describe full severity, frequency, duration, functional impact, and bad/flare days without exaggeration or minimization.
