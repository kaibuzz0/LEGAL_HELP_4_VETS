🚨 GET A LAWYER

Get counsel urgently when

  • A trial, appeal, writ, lockout, utility shutoff, or foreclosure deadline is close.
  • You are on active covered military service and SCRA may apply.
  • A disability accommodation may affect the housing dispute.
  • You live in subsidized or federally assisted housing.
  • Domestic violence, bankruptcy, title/ownership, probate, or foreclosure issues overlap the eviction.
