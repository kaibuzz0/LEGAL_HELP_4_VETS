🚨 GET A LAWYER

Get counsel urgently when

  • A trial, appeal, writ, lockout, utility interruption, foreclosure sale, or court-response date is close.
  • You have home-equity, tax, or HOA/POA foreclosure papers.
  • You are in covered military service and SCRA may apply.
  • A disability accommodation may affect the dispute.
  • You live in public, voucher, HUD-VASH, project-based, or other federally assisted housing.
  • Bankruptcy, domestic violence, probate, title, or ownership issues overlap the housing case.
