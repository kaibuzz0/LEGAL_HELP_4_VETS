# LEGAL HELP 4 VETS

A free, open-source, mobile-first legal and benefits help site for U.S. veterans, servicemembers, and their families.

**Live site:** https://kaibuzz0.github.io/LEGAL_HELP_4_VETS/

## What this is

LEGAL HELP 4 VETS is a static HTML site that turns complex federal, state, and local veteran benefits into plain-language, action-first guides. Every factual claim is backed by an official source listed in the [Sources Registry](https://kaibuzz0.github.io/LEGAL_HELP_4_VETS/sources.html).

The site is designed to work for veterans in crisis:
- No login, no cookies, no tracking.
- Works on a phone with poor service.
- Every page has a phone number you can tap to call.
- Every page can be printed as a one-page take-away.

## Methodology

- **Source-before-claim:** Every form number, phone number, address, and legal citation is sourced before it appears on a page.
- **Official sources preferred:** VA.gov, DFAS.mil, SSA.gov, USCIS.gov, HUD.gov, eCFR, Cornell LII, and official court sources.
- **No invented facts:** No success rates, no outcome promises, no “VA always denies X” claims.
- **Legal information, not legal advice:** The site does not replace an accredited VSO, attorney, or claims agent.

## How to use

1. Open the live site on a phone or computer.
2. Pick the page that matches the emergency or problem.
3. Follow the action ladder at the top.
4. Use the “When They Say No” section if you get stalled or denied.
5. Verify facts in the Sources Registry before relying on them for a formal claim.
