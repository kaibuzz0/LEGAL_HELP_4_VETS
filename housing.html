HOUSING: KEEP OR GET A ROOF

Housing problems move fast: eviction notices, utility shutoffs, foreclosure sales, and unsafe living conditions. This page gives veterans and families exact steps, phone numbers, and sample letters to stop the fall or get back inside.

SSVF Step-by-Step: Short-Term Help to Stay Housed or Leave Homelessness

Supportive Services for Veteran Families (SSVF) provides housing counseling, case management, and temporary financial help. [30]

  1. Call 1-877-4AID-VET or find your local SSVF provider through the VA homeless programs locator. [32]
  2. Say: "I need SSVF intake. I am a veteran [or family member] in [city, county] and I am homeless / at risk of homelessness within [days]. Can you schedule an intake or give me a referral?"
  3. Ask what documents are required. Common list: DD-214, photo ID, proof of income, lease/eviction notice or homelessness statement, utility bills, bank statements, household IDs, and a written budget.
  4. Attend intake. Be specific about arrears, barriers, and medical or behavioral health needs.
  5. If approved, ask exactly what SSVF can cover: past-due rent, security/utility deposits, moving costs, transportation, childcare, emergency supplies, and utility arrears. Ask how long assistance lasts.
  6. Complete any required follow-up appointments or case-management check-ins.

What if they say no: Ask why, get the denial in writing, ask if another local provider has funding, and call 1-877-4AID-VET back for a referral to a different SSVF grantee.

HUD-VASH Step-by-Step: Long-Term Voucher Plus VA Case Management

HUD-VASH combines a HUD housing voucher with VA supportive services, mainly for chronically homeless veterans. [31]

  1. Contact your local VA homeless program or call 1-877-4AID-VET.
  2. Complete a VA housing screen and assessment. Be honest about homelessness history, health conditions, and need for case management.
  3. If accepted, get a referral to the local housing authority for a HUD-VASH voucher.
  4. Use the voucher to lease an eligible unit. Your VA case manager stays involved for ongoing support.

Find the Nearest VA Homeless Program

Use the VA Homeless Programs locator at VA.gov or call 1-877-4AID-VET and give your ZIP code. Ask for the local homeless coordinator's direct number and email.

Script: "I am a veteran in ZIP code [XXXXX]. I need the contact information for the closest SSVF provider and VA homeless coordinator. Can you give me the phone number, address, and intake hours?"

Eviction / Foreclosure and SCRA Protections

Active-duty servicemembers have protections against eviction, foreclosure, and high interest rates under the SCRA. [35]

Sample SCRA stay/eviction request:

"Dear [Landlord/Court Clerk]: I am currently on active duty with [branch], serving at [location]. Under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act, 50 U.S.C. 3931 and 50 U.S.C. 3951, I request a stay of eviction / foreclosure proceedings. Attached is a copy of my active-duty orders. Please confirm receipt and the next court date. [Name, service details, contact]."

Where to send it: the landlord's attorney, the court clerk handling the eviction, or the mortgage servicer's loss mitigation department. Send certified mail and keep a copy.

VA Home Loan Delinquency / Forbearance

If you have a VA-backed loan and are falling behind, call your servicer immediately and ask for loss mitigation options. [51]

  1. Call the number on your mortgage statement. Ask for the "loss mitigation" or "home retention" department.
  2. Say: "I am a veteran with a VA loan. I am having trouble making payments because [reason]. What options do I have: forbearance, repayment plan, loan modification, or VA refinancing?"
  3. Request a VA Loan Technician by calling the VA Regional Loan Center for your area.
  4. Submit a complete loss mitigation application before any foreclosure deadline.
  5. If denied, ask for the reason in writing and consider a VA complaint and housing counselor.

Reasonable Accommodation Request Under the Fair Housing Act

42 U.S.C. 3604(f) prohibits housing discrimination based on disability and requires landlords to grant reasonable accommodations. [36]

Sample letter: "I am a tenant at [address]. I have a disability and need the following reasonable accommodation: [reserved accessible parking space / permission for an emotional support animal / transfer to a ground-floor unit / waiver of no-pet policy]. This request is made under the Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. 3604(f). I have attached [provider letter / medical documentation]. Please respond within 10 business days."

Emergency Shelter: 211 Script

Dial 211 (or search 211.org) for local shelter and housing referrals. [59]

Script: "I am a veteran [or family member] in [city/county]. I need emergency shelter tonight and short-term housing assistance. Can you tell me the nearest veteran-specific shelter, warming center, or emergency housing program? I have [number] people in my household, including [number] children."

Housing Document Checklist

  • DD-214 (any copy)
  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Current lease or mortgage statement
  • Eviction notice, foreclosure notice, or utility shutoff notice
  • Proof of income (pay stubs, VA benefits letter, unemployment)
  • Bank statements (last 1-2 months)
  • IDs / birth certificates / Social Security cards for all dependents
  • Written budget and list of monthly expenses
YOUR NEXT ACTION: If you are homeless or about to lose housing, call 1-877-4AID-VET now. If you have a VA loan in default, call your servicer's loss mitigation department today and request a VA Loan Technician.
