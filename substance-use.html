SUBSTANCE USE & RECOVERY

Substance use challenges do not make you less of a veteran, and they do not automatically destroy your benefits. What matters is getting safe help now and understanding your rights.

If You Need Help Right Now

  • Veterans Crisis Line: Call 988 and press 1, or text 838255. Confidential, 24/7, and you do not need to be enrolled in VA care. [1]
  • SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357 (HELP). Free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral and information for individuals and families facing substance use disorders. [2]

VA Treatment Options

VA provides mental health and substance use treatment services for enrolled veterans, including counseling, medication-assisted treatment, and residential programs. [3] Under the MISSION Act, VA Community Care can authorize treatment from community providers when appropriate. [4]

Benefits and Legal Issues

A history of substance use does not, by itself, automatically bar you from VA disability compensation. The key question under 38 C.F.R. § 3.301 is whether the disability is service-connected and not the result of willful misconduct. [5]

Many states and counties operate Veterans Treatment Courts. Programs and eligibility vary by jurisdiction, so contact your local court or a Veterans Service Organization for guidance. [6]

YOUR NEXT ACTION: If you are in crisis, call 988 and press 1. Otherwise, contact your VA primary care or mental health team to ask about substance use treatment options.
