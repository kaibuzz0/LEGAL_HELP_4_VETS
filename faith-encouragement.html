FAITH & ENCOURAGEMENT

This page offers Psalms for veterans and families who draw strength from faith. It is not legal advice, medical advice, or a substitute for crisis care.

Finding Chaplain and Pastoral Care Support

VA chaplains provide spiritual and pastoral care for veterans, families, and staff regardless of religious affiliation. [76]

  1. Contact your local VA medical center's chaplain service and ask to speak with a chaplain.
  2. Explain what you need: prayer, counseling, grief support, or help finding a faith community in your area.
  3. If you are not enrolled in VA health care, ask whether community chaplain or faith-based resources are available.
Script: "I am a veteran [or family member] looking for chaplain or pastoral care support. Can I speak with a chaplain or get a referral to a faith-based support resource in my area?"

Crisis Reminder

If you are in immediate crisis, faith encouragement is a supplement, not a replacement for emergency care:

  • Veterans Crisis Line: 988, press 1 [1]
  • SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357 [2]
  • Emergency: Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
