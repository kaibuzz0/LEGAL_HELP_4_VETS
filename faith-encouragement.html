FAITH & ENCOURAGEMENT

This page offers selected Psalms for veterans and families who draw strength from faith. These verses are encouragement, not legal advice, medical advice, or a substitute for crisis care. If you are in immediate crisis, call 988 and press 1 or reach out to the Veterans Crisis Line.

"The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters." Psalm 23:1–2 (KJV)

Available Verses

The banners across the site rotate among these King James Version Psalms. Each refresh may show a different verse.

  • Psalm 23:1–2 (KJV) — The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters.
  • Psalm 27:1 (KJV) — The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?
  • Psalm 46:1 (KJV) — God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
  • Psalm 91:1–2 (KJV) — He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
  • Psalm 121:1–2 (KJV) — I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help. My help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth.
  • Psalm 34:17–18 (KJV) — The righteous cry, and the LORD heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles. The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

About the Text

All verses are from the King James Version (KJV), first published in 1611. The KJV is in the public domain in the United States.[16]

No interpretation, testimony, or outcome is promised here. If these words help you stand steady, that is enough.
