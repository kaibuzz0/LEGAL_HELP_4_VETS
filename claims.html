CLAIMS BATTLE ROOM

The One Rule That Wins Claims

The VA does not know your story unless you build the record.

Your job is to make the connection between:

  1. Something that happened in service, and
  2. A current diagnosed condition, and
  3. A current level of disability.

If any of those three is weak, your claim is weak.

Evidence Checklist

Use this before filing any claim.

Service Records

  • DD-214
  • Service treatment records
  • Personnel file
  • Deployment orders
  • Awards and decorations
  • Unit records or after-action reports

Medical Records

  • VA treatment records
  • Private doctor records
  • Emergency room visits
  • Specialist opinions
  • Imaging, lab results, test reports

Personal Statements

  • Your lay statement (VA Form 21-4138)
  • Spouse or family member statement
  • Buddy statements from fellow service members
  • Employer or coworker statement about work limitations

Exposure and Event Documentation

  • Deployment locations and dates
  • Unit newsletters or records showing exposures
  • Photos, maps, incident reports
  • Newspaper articles or government reports about the event

Building the Nexus

The nexus is the medical link between service and your current condition. The VA standard is:

"At least as likely as not" the condition was caused or aggravated by service.

Get a medical opinion in that exact language.

Good nexus language:

"After reviewing the veteran's service treatment records and post-service treatment records, it is my opinion that it is at least as likely as not that [current condition] was caused by / aggravated by / secondary to the veteran's military service / service-connected [condition]."

Weak nexus language to avoid:

  • "Could be related."
  • "Might be from service."
  • "Possibly connected."

Filing the Claim

  1. Go to VA.gov or use a VSO.
  2. File VA Form 21-526EZ for initial claims.
  3. Keep a copy of everything submitted.
  4. Get confirmation of filing.
  5. Track your claim online or by calling 1-800-827-1000.

Protecting Your Effective Date

If a deadline is approaching and your evidence is not ready:

  • File a protective claim before the deadline.
  • Submit the full evidence as soon as possible.
  • The effective date may be worth thousands of dollars in back pay.
