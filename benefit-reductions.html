Legal Information, Not Legal Advice. Do not use one generic deadline for every VA payment change. The rule depends on what VA is changing and why.

VA Says Your Payment Will Change

DO THIS FIRST: find the VA notice, identify whether it says proposed or final, and identify the exact reason VA gives for changing the money.

Last legally verified: 2026-08-27
Primary authorities: 38 CFR §§ 3.105, 3.343, 3.344, 3.951, 3.957, 3.665, 3.353; current VA decision-review guidance.
Important: dependency, pension, debt, incarceration, severance, rating reduction, and incompetency are different procedures.

⚠️ DEADLINE

First question: proposal or final decision?

If VA is using the proposal procedure in 38 CFR § 3.105(d)–(h), the notice generally gives 60 days to present evidence. The same regulation provides a 30-day period to request a predetermination hearing for those specified proposed actions. A timely hearing request under § 3.105(i) continues the existing benefit-payment level pending the final determination.

Do not generalize this. Section 3.105 itself contains exceptions, and other payment changes use other rules or effective dates. Read the actual notice.

🛠️ PRACTICAL MOVE

Save the notice and envelope/electronic receipt; calendar both the evidence deadline and any hearing-request deadline separately. Keep proof of delivery for anything you submit.

⚖️ YOUR RIGHT

Disability evaluation reduction

When a lower service-connected evaluation or employability status would reduce compensation currently being paid, 38 CFR § 3.105(e) requires a proposal stating material facts and reasons and generally gives 60 days to submit evidence. If the predetermination-hearing provisions apply, the hearing request period is 30 days.

Check for additional protection before accepting the reduction

  • Rating at the same level 5+ years: 38 CFR § 3.344 adds stabilization protections. VA must review the full history; an examination less full and complete than the examination supporting the existing rating is not a proper reduction basis under the regulation, and certain episodic conditions require evidence of sustained improvement.
  • Total rating / TDIU: 38 CFR § 3.343 adds separate improvement and employability safeguards. For TDIU reductions, actual employability must be established by clear and convincing evidence under § 3.343(c).
  • Evaluation in effect 20+ years: 38 CFR § 3.951 generally protects the evaluation from reduction below that level except fraud.

📋 CHECKLIST Compare the examination used to propose reduction with the examination/history supporting the existing rating. Gather treatment records, lay evidence about ordinary-life functioning, work limitations, flare patterns, and evidence showing whether any improvement is sustained.

🚨 GET ACCREDITED HELP

Service-connection severance is not the same as a rating reduction

Under 38 CFR § 3.105(d), VA bears the burden to establish that the grant of service connection was clearly and unmistakably erroneous, subject to protection rules. A proposed severance must state the material facts and reasons and generally gives 60 days for evidence.

10-year protection: under 38 CFR § 3.957, service connection in effect 10 or more years generally may not be severed except for fraud or the specific military-service/character-of-discharge exception stated in the regulation.

🛠️ PRACTICAL MOVE Identify whether VA is disputing the original factual basis, diagnosis, service records, nexus, or legal eligibility. Answer the exact severance theory rather than submitting generic evidence.

⚠️ DEADLINE

Dependency or marital-status change

38 CFR § 3.105(h) contains a proposal procedure for many reductions based on dependency, marital status, income, net worth, or other status, but it expressly has exceptions and its effective-date rules point to §§ 3.500–3.503. Do not assume the disability-rating reduction effective-date rule applies.

Find the event VA says changed eligibility—marriage, divorce, death, child status, school attendance, or another dependency fact—and gather the official records that prove the correct date and status.

⚠️ DEADLINE

Pension income, net-worth, or employability change

Pension can change for reasons that are legally different from a disability-compensation rating reduction. Section 3.105(f) addresses disability/employability-based pension reduction, while § 3.105(h) addresses many income/net-worth/status changes. Follow the reason in the notice and the effective-date rule VA cites.

Gather income records, unreimbursed medical expenses when relevant, net-worth documentation, dependency records, and the VA calculations you dispute.

📚 AUTHORITY

Incarceration reduction

38 CFR § 3.665 is its own pathway. For covered felony incarceration, compensation or DIC is limited beginning on the 61st day. The regulation also requires notice about possible apportionment to dependents and addresses restoration after release.

Important evidence: conviction offense/classification, incarceration start date, release/work-release/halfway-house/parole dates, dependent information, and proof of release. If a conviction is overturned, § 3.665 contains a restoration rule.

📄 NOTICE CONTROLS

Debt, offset, or withholding

A debt-collection withholding is not automatically the same thing as reducing the underlying disability evaluation. Open the debt notice and identify: whether VA disputes entitlement, whether VA created an overpayment, what collection method is proposed, and what dispute/waiver/payment-plan deadlines the notice gives.

Open the VA Debt pathway. If VA is also changing the underlying benefit entitlement, use that benefit-change route too.

⚖️ YOUR RIGHT

Proposed incompetency

Under 38 CFR § 3.353, VA competency is about capacity to manage affairs and benefit funds; it is not the same thing as a disability percentage. The regulation presumes competency when reasonable doubt exists. For a proposed incompetency finding, VA ordinarily must notify the beneficiary and provide a hearing opportunity under § 3.103 before the determination, subject to specified court-based exceptions.

Gather medical evidence specifically addressing capacity to manage funds/affairs, evidence of actual financial management, and any evidence VA relied on. A hearing requested under § 3.353 must occur before the incompetency rating decision.

🛠️ PRACTICAL MOVE

Other administrative or eligibility change

  1. Do not force the case into the rating-reduction rules just because the dollar amount goes down.
  2. Find the exact regulation or authority printed in the notice.
  3. Separate the entitlement decision from any later debt collection.
  4. Use the review-rights notice after a final decision to identify the authorized review route.
  5. Get VA-accredited help when the loss is substantial or the notice is unclear.
📋 CHECKLIST

Build the response around VA's stated reason

  1. Mark PROPOSED or FINAL.
  2. Write down the notice date and actual receipt date.
  3. Identify exactly what is changing: evaluation, service connection, dependency, pension, incarceration payment, debt collection, competency, or another status.
  4. Copy the factual reason VA gives.
  5. Copy the regulation or authority VA cites.
  6. Calendar each separate evidence, hearing, or review deadline.
  7. Gather evidence that directly disproves the stated reason.
  8. If a predetermination hearing is available and useful, make the request within the notice/rule deadline.
  9. Preserve complete copies and proof of submission.
  10. After a final decision, follow the review-rights notice for the appropriate review lane.
